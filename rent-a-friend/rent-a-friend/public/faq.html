Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on safety, pricing and how it works.

Is this a dating service?

No. Rent A Friend is for platonic companionship such as coffee, walks, events and conversation. Sexual services and romantic solicitation are prohibited.

Who can use it?

Adults aged 18 or over only.

How is a friend verified?

Every application is reviewed by our team before it is listed. Profiles marked Sample are fictional examples.

How does pricing work?

Each friend sets an hourly rate. You see the full total, including any service fee, before confirming.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Draft policy: free cancellation more than 24 hours before the start time. Final terms need legal review.

What if I feel unsafe?

Leave, move to a public place and contact local emergency services (India: 112). Then report the profile.
