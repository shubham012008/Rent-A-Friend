Why join

  • Flexible: set your own days and hours.
  • Fair: you set your hourly rate; the platform fee is shown upfront (10%).
  • Safe: block, report and boundary tools.

How earning works

Guests book and pay through the platform. After a completed booking, your earnings minus the fee are paid out. Payouts need a payment provider and are not live yet.
